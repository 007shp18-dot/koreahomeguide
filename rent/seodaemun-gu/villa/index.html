RENTING IN SEODAEMUN-GU

What renters should know about villa and low-rise homes in Seodaemun-gu

Seodaemun is shaped by university and young-professional demand around Yonsei University, Ewha Womans University, Sinchon, and residential areas toward Hongje. Line 2, Line 3, and dense bus routes make the district practical for renters who want access to central and western Seoul. Small units near campuses can price differently from quieter residential pockets.

Villa and low-rise housing can offer more varied prices and layouts than large apartment complexes. Building age, renovation quality, elevator access, security, and deposit protection deserve extra attention, so compare the exact building and contract rather than relying only on a district average.

Use signed transactions as a market reference, then check the exact unit’s maintenance fees, floor area, building condition, station access, and contract protections before committing.

How to use this page

Start with rent by deposit, compare similar floor-area groups, then use Rent Check for the exact quote you received. Official transaction data is a reference point, not a property appraisal.
