What this official data shows

What it cannot show

Official transactions do not capture every difference in floor, condition, furnishings, management fees, view, exact location or contract negotiation. Use the data as a market reference, not an appraisal.

RENTING IN GANGNAM-GU

What renters should know about apartments in Gangnam-gu

For renters working around Gangnam Station, Teheran-ro, COEX, Samsung, or southern Seoul, the district can reduce commute time substantially. Line 2, the Shinbundang Line, Line 9, and multiple bus corridors make it practical for office workers who value direct access more than maximum floor area.

The trade-off is price. A higher monthly rent can buy newer buildings, shorter walks to major stations, better building management, and more predictable amenities. Moving only a few stations away can change the rent-to-space equation quickly, so comparing the exact property type and size is more useful than relying on a single “Gangnam average.”

Apartments are typically the most standardized residential option, with clearer building management, security, elevators, and shared facilities than many smaller buildings. They often suit renters who value predictable building quality, family-sized layouts, parking, and resale-style residential neighborhoods. The downside is that deposits can be larger and small-unit supply may be limited in some districts.

Check maintenance fees carefully, especially in newer officetels and mixed-use towers. Furnished units, parking, floor level, and building age can create large differences even when two contracts have similar deposits and floor area. When comparing apartment contracts, pay attention to exclusive-use area, building age, floor, renovation condition, station distance, and whether the contract is monthly rent or jeonse. A nominally similar apartment can still differ materially because of view, floor, or interior condition.

FAQ

Questions about this rental market

How current is the data on this page?

The page uses the latest six completed contract months available through the official feed and displays the data-through month after loading.

Does one median combine every deposit and home size?

No. Monthly rent is shown with deposit bands and floor-area groups so unlike contract structures are not collapsed into one synthetic quote.

Why can two Gangnam apartments with similar sizes have different rents?

Floor, building age, renovation, view, station distance, parking, furnishings and exact deposit terms can create meaningful differences.
