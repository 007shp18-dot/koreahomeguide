RENTING IN GWANGJIN-GU

What renters should know about officetels in Gwangjin-gu

Gwangjin attracts students and young professionals around Konkuk University and Sejong University, with strong access around Guui and Gangbyeon. Line 2 and Line 7 connect the district to Gangnam and eastern Seoul, creating demand for compact apartments, officetels, and low-rise homes. Deposit levels can change substantially by station access and building age.

Officetels are common among students and single professionals because they often provide compact layouts near stations and commercial areas. Check maintenance fees carefully: two units with similar rent can have very different total monthly costs once building charges are included.

Use signed transactions as a market reference, then check the exact unit’s maintenance fees, floor area, building condition, station access, and contract protections before committing.

How to use this page
