What this official data shows

This page uses reported MOLIT rental transactions from the latest six completed months. Monthly rent is grouped with its deposit and floor area, while zero-rent jeonse contracts remain separate.

What it cannot show

Official transactions do not capture every difference in floor, condition, furnishings, management fees, view, exact location or contract negotiation. Use the data as a market reference, not an appraisal.

RENTING IN YONGSAN-GU

What renters should know about low-rise homes in Yongsan-gu

The district is useful for people who need flexible access to central Seoul, the south side of the Han River, or major railway connections at Yongsan Station. Depending on the neighborhood, subway convenience can be excellent or surprisingly uneven, so station walking time matters.

Price dispersion is unusually wide. International demand, luxury housing, older villas, compact officetels, and redevelopment-era stock can all appear within the same district. A district median is useful as a starting point, but property type, floor area, and exact sub-neighborhood should always be checked.

This page covers Korea's rowhouse and multi-household low-rise category (연립·다세대). These buildings can offer more space or a quieter residential setting, but they are less standardized than large apartment complexes. Detached and multi-family houses (단독·다가구), which include many one-room-style rentals, are a separate official transaction category and are not mixed into these figures.

Do not assume a familiar expat neighborhood means a contract is automatically renter-friendly. Verify the registered owner, exact address, deposit protection options, maintenance fees, and whether furniture or utilities that appear in a listing are actually written into the contract. Inspect the building in person, confirm the exact registered address and owner, check for moisture or mold, review security and parking, and understand deposit-protection options. Condition can vary substantially even between nearby buildings.

How to use this page

Start with the deposit-band rent section, then compare floor-area groups and your exact quote with KoreaHomeGuide Rent Check. The official transaction dataset does not capture every qualitative feature, so use the result to challenge or validate an asking price rather than treating it as an appraisal.

FAQ

Questions about this rental market

How current is the data on this page?

The page uses the latest six completed contract months available through the official feed and displays the data-through month after loading.

Does one median combine every deposit and home size?

No. Monthly rent is shown with deposit bands and floor-area groups so unlike contract structures are not collapsed into one synthetic quote.

Why can nearby Yongsan villa or low-rise homes differ sharply?

Building condition, exact street, floor, elevator, parking, sunlight, renovation and management arrangements may differ even at a similar size.
