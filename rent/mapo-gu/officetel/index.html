Official transactions do not capture every difference in floor, condition, furnishings, management fees, view, exact location or contract negotiation. Use the data as a market reference, not an appraisal.

RENTING IN MAPO-GU

What renters should know about officetels in Mapo-gu

Airport Railroad access, Line 2, Line 6, and connections toward central Seoul make Mapo attractive to students, creative-industry workers, and newcomers who want nightlife or airport access without living south of the river. The same district includes very different micro-markets around Hongdae, Gongdeok, Mangwon, and residential hills.

Renters often trade newer construction for neighborhood character and location. Villas can offer more space for the money than newer officetels, while apartments may command a premium for security, elevators, parking, and standardized management. Comparing reported contracts by housing type is therefore important.

Officetels are common among single professionals and newcomers because they are often near stations, compact, secure, and easier to find with modern facilities or furnishings. They can be convenient for a first Seoul rental, especially when commute time matters more than maximum space. However, usable floor area can feel smaller than the headline number and monthly management fees are often more important than renters initially expect.

Older villas may have weaker soundproofing, limited parking, steep access roads, or larger differences between advertised and actual condition. Around busy nightlife streets, inspect noise at the time you would normally be at home and confirm every management-fee item before signing. Compare maintenance fees, included utilities, parking rules, residential-use status, building age, and furnished items. Two officetels with the same deposit and area can have very different all-in monthly costs once management fees are included.

How to use this page

Start with the deposit-band rent section, then compare floor-area groups and your exact quote with KoreaHomeGuide Rent Check. The official transaction dataset does not capture every qualitative feature, so use the result to challenge or validate an asking price rather than treating it as an appraisal.

FAQ

Questions about this rental market

How current is the data on this page?

The page uses the latest six completed contract months available through the official feed and displays the data-through month after loading.

Does one median combine every deposit and home size?

No. Monthly rent is shown with deposit bands and floor-area groups so unlike contract structures are not collapsed into one synthetic quote.

Does the official rent include officetel management fees?

No. Ask for an itemized management fee and recent bills because utilities, parking and shared-service charges vary by building and unit.
