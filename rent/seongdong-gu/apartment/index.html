RENTING IN SEONGDONG-GU

What renters should know about apartments in Seongdong-gu

Wangsimni is a major transport hub, while Seongsu gives fast access to eastern Gangnam and the Line 2 office corridor. Renters who work in Seongsu, Jamsil, Gangnam, or central Seoul often choose the district to balance commute and neighborhood atmosphere.

Newer officetels and renovated buildings near Seongsu can price very differently from older residential streets only a short walk away. Apartments may offer more predictable building services, while villas can provide more area at the cost of older facilities or less standardized management.

Apartments are typically the most standardized residential option, with clearer building management, security, elevators, and shared facilities than many smaller buildings. They often suit renters who value predictable building quality, family-sized layouts, parking, and resale-style residential neighborhoods. The downside is that deposits can be larger and small-unit supply may be limited in some districts.

Seongsu’s popularity can create a premium that is not evenly distributed across the district. Check the actual walking route to the station, nearby construction, commercial noise, building-use classification, and management-fee breakdown rather than paying based on the neighborhood name alone. When comparing apartment contracts, pay attention to exclusive-use area, building age, floor, renovation condition, station distance, and whether the contract is monthly rent or jeonse. A nominally similar apartment can still differ materially because of view, floor, or interior condition.
