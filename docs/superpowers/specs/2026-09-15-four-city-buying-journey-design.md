# Four-city buying journey

Approved in conversation: budget-led homepage mockup, corrected so Seoul, Singapore, Dubai and Tokyo have equal initial visibility; user requested a roadmap and implementation and added SEED as the design reference.

## Experience
The homepage starts without a preselected city. Four equally sized city photo controls appear in Seoul, Singapore, Dubai, Tokyo order. Choosing a city reveals its own currency, three supported budget bands, dated historical transaction examples, evidence links, and contextual costs/price-research actions. The user can change city without carrying an amount into another currency. No fake listings, invented property photos, fabricated expertise, or promised brokerage service. Tokyo records remain anonymous neighbourhood examples, with one record per budget checkpoint rather than three supposedly equivalent projects.

Existing blue/white semantic tokens, sans-serif typography, minimum 44px targets, 48px controls, 8px control radii. Follow SEED Chip/Field/Action Button principles: visible labels, subtle selected surfaces plus accessible state, consistent pressed/hover/focus treatment, wrapping controls on mobile, no decorative blue sidelines. Keep photo credits and identify city photos as illustrative location images.

## Structure
A server-only model projects existing reviewed guide data into small serializable summaries. A focused client component owns city/budget selection; the homepage shell renders editorial and trust context. Full transaction records stay out of the homepage client payload. Existing guide routes and raw evidence routes remain authoritative. Guide links carry supported budget selection where the guide is interactive. Existing budget guides and cost assumptions remain intact.

Insights default to buying decisions, retaining neighbourhood content in an explicit collection and preserving published URLs. Primary navigation prioritizes Explore, Buying guides and Insights; Community remains reachable in More and footer. Trust/contact explain evidence scope and available research support, with no invented operator identity, partners, response time, results or credentials.
