# SignedPrice Three-Market Pre-AdSense Program

**Goal:** Turn the current Seoul-heavy set of tools into one coherent three-city decision service, then complete the original-content and policy foundation required before AdSense is enabled.

## Execution order

| Gate | Plan | User-visible outcome | Release rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `2026-09-04-signedprice-global-property-core.md` | Truthful city capabilities and shared identity/provenance | Dual-read parity; no legacy deletion |
| 2 | `2026-09-04-signedprice-three-city-home-navigation.md` | Three-city home with city-specific evidence and actions | Responsive browser review approved |
| 3 | `2026-09-04-signedprice-property-media-detail.md` | Real approved photos first; Street View optional | Rights and identity gates pass |
| 4 | `2026-09-04-signedprice-connected-market-journeys.md` | Explore → Detail → Check → evidence return paths | Complete journeys verified per city |
| 5 | `2026-09-04-signedprice-pre-adsense-content.md` | 34 English and 12 Chinese originals connected to tools | Crawl, policy, CWV, and editorial review pass |

## Product invariants across every gate

- Seoul, Singapore, and Dubai remain visible; different data depth is disclosed rather than disguised.
- Language selection is separate from market selection.
- A number never appears without market, sector, native currency, period, source, sample/coverage, and limitation context.
- A page never links to a market action that its capability model marks unavailable or rights-blocked.
- Exact-property photography requires verified identity and display rights; Street View is never the primary image.
- Explore state survives detail navigation, and Detail passes verified entity context into Check.
- Guides and Insights may reserve advertising positions; Explore, Detail evidence, and Check decision areas do not.
- No AdSense script is enabled in this program. The final gate produces an approval-ready site, not an automatic approval claim.

## Review checkpoints

1. Approve the interactive screen concept and copy hierarchy.
2. Approve the real Home captures after Gate 2.
3. Approve the four media fallback states after Gate 3.
4. Complete one Seoul and one Singapore end-to-end decision journey; verify Dubai's honest overview path.
5. Review representative English/Chinese articles, policy pages, sitemap, and mobile ad placeholders before Production promotion.
