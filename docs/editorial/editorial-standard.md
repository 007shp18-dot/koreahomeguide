# SignedPrice editorial standard

A reader should leave an article with a better decision, not merely another list of things to investigate. State the question and the best supported answer early. Length is earned by evidence, an explanation or a useful calculation. Do not impose a minimum word count or a fixed number of headings.

## Columns and market analysis

- Begin with one tension or decision: advertised yield versus collected cash, cheaper purchase versus future obligations, headline price index versus the buyer’s particular segment.
- Explain why it matters now using a dated, relevant source. News, a quoted participant’s claim and SignedPrice’s inference must remain distinguishable.
- Show the mechanism. Disaggregate an average or walk through a calculation rather than stacking general observations.
- Compare like units and disclose differences: dates, geography, asset type, tenure, size, sample, denominator and exclusions. A median of building medians is not a transaction-weighted city median.
- Include the strongest condition or evidence that changes the answer. A buyer’s holding period, financing, cash needs or document gap can reverse a conclusion.
- End at the next useful decision or data view. Avoid a second generic summary repeating the opening.

## Guides

Specify who the guide is for and the decision it answers. For each important step name the actual document, field or input; explain what the result changes. Replace “check management” with the account, payment schedule and unresolved funding question. A worked example is better than repeated instructions to calculate.

A policy explainer must state the applicable rule, effective date, scope and significant exceptions. Do not label an internal copy edit as a policy change. If current applicability cannot be established, narrow the page’s claim and identify the gap. A generic instruction to check an official site does not fulfil a title promising current rates.

## Evidence and calculations

Use original statistics, enacted rules, official service pages or the research publisher’s full report where available. Read the relevant table and notes. A search snippet is a lead, not enough to certify a numerical table. PDFs need a page reference in the research ledger; record inaccessible sources without borrowing their numbers from secondary summaries.

Store publisher, original URL, publication/effective date where known, observation period and actual check date. A site footer is not a report’s publication date. Official releases can contain inconsistent totals or translation errors: reconcile them or omit the conflicting claim.

Label a scenario once next to its inputs and maintain the distinction at the conclusion. Recompute totals from unrounded inputs; disclose rounding only where material. Separate purchase-price yield, yield on acquisition cash, operating income and cash after debt service. Avoid double-counting reserve contributions, special assessments, insurance or principal/interest. Cumulative cash is not investment return when residual asset value and opportunity cost are excluded.

Keep citations close to the claims they support. Use one visible citation mechanism per claim plus a final source list. Never remove a unique source while reducing visual repetition. A national survey is not a current quote for a Tokyo unit; a community rent index is not a lease forecast for a Dubai apartment.

## Reporting integrity and language

Never fabricate an interview, resident, quotation, visit or verified property document. Reddit identifies questions and competing claims; it does not verify prices, law or returns. Distinguish a discussion teaser from a completed analysis. Write natural Korean and Chinese, with equivalent evidence and numerical meaning rather than literal hedge-heavy translation. Preserve routes and publication history through revision.
