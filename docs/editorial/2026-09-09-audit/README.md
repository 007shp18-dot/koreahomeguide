# Existing content audit — 9 September 2026

The review read the resolved static portfolio, including research expansions, and the independent city articles. **67 distinct article subjects** are represented by **140 localized bodies**: 37 portfolio groups / 80 language records, plus 30 bilingual journey articles / 60 bodies. An additional four bilingual city landing stories and 20 bilingual discussion teasers were reviewed. Thirty-four redirected legacy guide/insight routes were inspected and excluded from article counts. Live third-party news-feed entries were outside this static-column/guide audit.

This is an editorial audit, not a certification that every retained source was independently fetched today. The separate evidence memos identify the sources actually retrieved and the scope of verification. Do not equate the audit date with a new fact-check date on every article.

## Findings

| Portfolio | Keep | Tighten | Rewrite | Merge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 37 article groups | 4 | 17 | 10 | 6 |
| 30 city articles + 4 city hubs + 20 teasers | 18 | 17 | 12 | 7 |

The second row includes short teasers and navigation hubs, so these action counts are not additional independent articles. Each JSON record records the concrete issue, what should be preserved and any data required. The tables are the pre-edit snapshot.

The recurring defect is a missing answer, not length alone. Policy stubs defer to an official page without stating the applicable rule. Some area guides tell the reader how to compare but never provide the promised comparison. Worked cost models and reproducible budget screens have substance, but their conclusions are buried under repeated warnings and methods.
