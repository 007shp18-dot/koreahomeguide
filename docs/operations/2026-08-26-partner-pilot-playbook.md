# KoreaHomeGuide First Partner Pilot Playbook

**Status:** Candidate discovery only. No partner is approved, contacted or displayed by this document.  
**Objective:** Test whether a value-first Rent Check can create a safe, attributable paid introduction without weakening user trust.

## Category decision

| Candidate | Product fit | Operational/legal risk | Pilot position |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Licensed, genuinely foreigner-capable brokerage | Highest; closest to the signing decision | Highest | Preferred only after legal review and every launch gate below |
| Telecom/SIM/home internet provider | Medium; useful after move-in | Lower, but identity/billing and reseller boundaries remain | Default fallback first pilot if brokerage compensation is not cleared |
| Broad relocation/settlement service | Medium, but scope varies | High manual service and quality variance | Later; narrow the service before testing |

KoreaHomeGuide should interview candidates in all three categories. It must not present an operator as “verified,” “best,” “safe” or “foreigner-friendly” merely because the operator says so.

## Pilot boundary

The initial product is a **user-consented introduction**, not brokerage or concierge delivery by KoreaHomeGuide.

KoreaHomeGuide will not:

- carry listings or negotiate a lease;
- calculate or collect a broker commission, rent, deposit or reservation payment;
- review a contract as legal advice;
- receive passport, ARC, bank or signed-contract images;
- guarantee price, property safety, deposit recovery, visa eligibility or service outcome; or
- share contact information before a separate, explicit introduction consent.

The existing ungated Rent Check and guidance remain available whether the user accepts or declines the introduction.

## Mandatory launch gates

All gates are required before a partner CTA appears on the site.

1. **Legal scope:** Korean counsel reviews the exact compensation, disclosure, role boundary and contract.
2. **Business verification:** legal name, representative, office address and current registration match independent official records.
3. **Accountable operator:** a named licensed/responsible professional and escalation contact own referred cases.
4. **Language test:** a live mystery-shop confirms the specific supported language, response quality, fee explanation and refusal of unsafe requests.
5. **Written consumer terms:** service area, fees, cancellation, complaint path and payment destination are clear before work starts.
6. **Data agreement:** recipient, purpose, shared fields, retention period, deletion, onward-transfer restriction and incident notice are documented.
7. **Safety code:** no payment to personal accounts, pressure tactics, undisclosed conflicts or unsupported protection claims.
8. **Pause right:** KoreaHomeGuide can immediately suspend introductions after a credible serious complaint or verification failure.

## Minimum pilot

- one partner;
- one limited Seoul service area;
- English plus only languages actually tested;
- at most ten consented introductions in the first cohort;
- a unique referral ID, without exposing it to analytics as PII;
- a defined initial-consultation milestone;
- manual reconciliation and user follow-up;
- immediate pause on a severe complaint.

Do not promise a fee, cashback or discount until it is contractually agreed, operationally deliverable and legally reviewed.

## Disclosure at the CTA

> **Pilot partner disclosure:** KoreaHomeGuide may receive compensation if you choose to use this partner. You can use any provider, and declining will not affect KoreaHomeGuide. KoreaHomeGuide is not your broker and does not receive or hold rent or deposits. We share your details only after you request an introduction.

Replace “may receive compensation” with the precise approved relationship when the commercial agreement is known.

## User benefit

Every introduced user should receive a benefit that does not depend on signing:

- a named, language-tested contact;
- transparent service-area and fee information;
- a translated viewing and contract-question checklist; and
- a KoreaHomeGuide follow-up route for service feedback.

## Pilot scorecard

| Area | Measure |
| --- | --- |
| Demand | Eligible result views, partner CTA views, consented introduction rate, decline reason |
| Delivery | Contact success, first-response time, attended consultation rate, duplicate/no-show rate |
| Trust | Disclosure comprehension, user satisfaction, opt-out/deletion requests |
| Safety | Complaints per introduction, severe incidents, fee/payment clarity, resolution time |
| Revenue | Recognized qualified introductions, milestone completions, cash received |
| Unit economics | Cash received minus acquisition, support, translation, refund and remediation cost |

Positive contribution is not enough to continue if trust or safety deteriorates.

## Stop conditions

Immediately pause new introductions for:

- suspected unlicensed activity or mismatched registration;
- misuse or unauthorized onward transfer of user data;
- misleading fee, deposit or payment instructions;
- coercion, discrimination, intimidation or a credible safety allegation;
- repeated missed response/service commitments; or
- a serious complaint that remains unresolved past the agreed escalation window.
