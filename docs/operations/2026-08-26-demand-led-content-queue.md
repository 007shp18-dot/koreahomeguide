**Date:** 2026-08-26  
**Status:** Active operating rule for roadmap Track B

## Decision

The homepage keeps three guide cards and links to the complete seven-guide hub. During the first 14-day validation window, publish no new indexable SEO URL. Use real search queries, community replies, support questions, and partner interviews to decide what deserves a page.

This prevents the site from repeating its earlier crawl-expansion problem while still turning user questions into a usable editorial backlog.

## Current homepage selection

1. How to rent in Korea as a foreigner: broad first-time-renter intent.
2. Wolse vs Jeonse: explains the market structure foreign renters must understand.
3. Seoul officetel rent: connects a common property search to deposits, rent, management fees, and hidden costs.

The existing before-you-sign pillar remains prominent in the secondary action immediately above the guide grid, so repeating it in the three-card grid would waste one of the limited discovery slots.

## Day-14 publishing rule

After the first 14-day review, ship at most two editorial changes in one cycle:

- one data-backed refresh to an existing page with meaningful impressions and weak CTR, clicks without Rent Check starts, incomplete answers evidenced by recurring questions, or clear user friction; and
- one new page only when the same unanswered question independently recurs at least three times and no existing page can satisfy it without changing its primary intent.

If no question clears that threshold, refresh two existing pages instead. Never publish to satisfy a weekly page quota.

## First linkable asset after evidence

The first larger editorial/data asset should be a transparent Seoul Rent Snapshot, not another thin district page. It should publish reproducible aggregates, coverage dates, sample sizes, property-type boundaries, known limitations, and a clear path into Rent Check. Build it only after the 14-day review identifies which districts, property types, and questions users actually care about.

## Post-Day-14 weekly operating loop

1. Export Search Console page/query evidence and GA4 funnel events using the existing measurement handoff.
2. Add normalized recurring questions from communities, email, and interviews.
3. Diagnose the earliest failing stage: discovery, click, tool start, result, or follow-up.
4. Fix that stage before adding more traffic or pages.
5. If an evidence gate is met, refresh one page or answer one validated question; otherwise keep observing.
6. Distribute answer-first in at most two contextually relevant placements with tracked links.
7. Record completed Rent Checks and follow-up actions, not sessions alone.

## Monetization boundary

- Product tools and first-viewport trust content stay ad-free.
- Existing dormant content-ad mounts remain inactive until traffic and policy readiness justify a controlled AdSense test.
- No partner promotion is added before a real partner passes the documented pilot gates.
- Any future referral placement must disclose compensation and explain the selection standard.
- Content decisions are not sold to advertisers or partners.
