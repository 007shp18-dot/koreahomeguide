# External Acquisition Kit

Status: Drafts for owner review only. Do not post, send, or submit automatically.

KoreaHomeGuide is not a listings service or broker. It uses recent official signed rental transactions to help a renter assess a quote and prepare better contract questions.

## Owner review boundary

Review the destination, context, recipient or thread, and final wording before any manual placement. These drafts are reference material only; they do not authorize outreach, submission, account access, or automated posting.

### Draft 1

Wolse and jeonse are not simply “rent” versus “free housing.” Wolse trades a smaller deposit for monthly rent; jeonse removes most monthly rent but puts far more deposit capital at risk. Compare cash need and protection steps before choosing.

https://koreahomeguide.com/guides/wolse-vs-jeonse/?utm_source=reddit&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=wolse_jeonse

### Draft 2

For a foreign renter, the Korea apartment process is easier when handled in order: total budget, housing type, quote comparison, property and owner checks, written contract terms, then move-in registration steps.

https://koreahomeguide.com/guides/rent-apartment-korea-foreigner/?utm_source=university_outreach&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=foreign_renter_process

### Draft 3

Before paying a large Korean rental deposit, make the property, registered owner, contract, receiving account and protection timeline form one consistent chain. Urgency is not a substitute for verification.

https://koreahomeguide.com/guides/before-you-sign/?utm_source=expat_community&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=deposit_safety

### Draft 4

An officetel's headline rent is only part of the cost. Compare the deposit, monthly rent, itemized management fee, usable area, registered use and contract terms together.

https://koreahomeguide.com/guides/seoul-officetel-rent/?utm_source=relocation_outreach&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=officetel_costs
