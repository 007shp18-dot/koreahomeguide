# SignedPrice: three-city roadmap and promotion drafts

Updated: 7 September 2026. These are proposed operating priorities and draft copy, not evidence of traffic or external publication.

## Product direction

Help readers move from a budget question to transaction evidence and then to a specific property check. Seoul, Singapore and Dubai remain first-class destinations, with the available evidence stated separately for each. A completed historical sale is not an available listing; a Dubai area comparison is not a unit rental appraisal.

Dubai is included now with area evidence, asking-price context and a transparent cost model. Expanding to unit-level rental claims requires the corresponding reliable data; the current illustrative yield model must remain labelled.

## Measurement

Existing Vercel analytics is enabled only where configured. Editorial attribution contains content ID, content type, locale, market and destination family. Do not send address, quoted price, search text or user identity in these events.

- Article entry: article_open on the homepage research cards.
- Article-to-product: article_to_explore and article_to_check on eligible internal links.
- Reading completion: existing article_complete markers are observational proxies, not proof the article was read.
- Tool use and sharing: retain existing tool_start, tool_complete and result_link_copy events.
- Report aggregate steps by city and campaign. These events alone do not establish an individual user's complete funnel; do not claim cross-session conversion without an appropriate measured definition.

Check the production analytics dashboard after real visits before treating instrumentation as confirmed event delivery. Run distribution for an initial two-week observation period, then compare city/source results with their denominators. Avoid declaring a winner from a handful of visits.

### Dubai — English

A 7% rental headline can become 4.32% on the cash you actually put in.

In our worked example, an AED 1 million apartment with AED 70,000 annual rent leaves about AED 45,958 after one vacant month and modeled owner costs. Against AED 1.065 million of purchase cash, that is 4.32% before financing and tax.

Change the service-charge assumption from AED 12,000 to AED 18,000 and it becomes 3.75%. See all nine vacancy and service-charge scenarios, plus the official fee sources. This is an illustrative calculation, not a Dubai market yield.
