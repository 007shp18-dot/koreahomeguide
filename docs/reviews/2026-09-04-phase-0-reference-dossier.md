## SignedPrice composition rules

### Homepage

- The first viewport answers one question: “Can this price or contract be checked against reported evidence?”
- Check is the only filled action. Explore is a text action.
- Evidence status and one lead analysis appear before guide navigation.
- No partner logos, investment lead form, unavailable market tiles, or fake popularity ranking.

### Content

- One report leads; the rest form a ruled editorial list rather than a card wall.
- Article headers carry market, published/updated dates, and `SignedPrice Data Desk` before the body.
- Source and comparison boundary sit beside the claim they qualify.
- Ads are visibly separate from evidence and never precede the first substantive paragraph.

### Check

- Inputs follow the actual decision order in one vertical flow.
- Result order is verdict → scope → key figures → sample/period → comparable evidence → disclosure.
- Insufficient evidence replaces figures; it does not render zeroes or an estimated verdict.

### Explore

- Desktop uses a 420px result rail and the remaining space for the first-party Seoul district map.
- Search and four core filters precede count, sort, evidence rows, and the selected-building action.
- Mobile edits the task into List and Map modes instead of shrinking the desktop split view.

## Content inventory boundary

- Homepage Guide links must come from the checked-in `GUIDES` inventory.
- English Insights must come from published editorial records, including the checked-in fallback only when the content store is unavailable.
- The Simplified Chinese piece in Phase 0 is visibly labelled as a non-public design sample and contains no numeric, legal, tax, price, or investment claim.
- Existing English articles are not bulk-translated to make the Chinese library appear larger.

## Rejected default patterns

- Full-bleed gradient heroes, glass panels, decorative blur, and rounded card mosaics.
- Flags as language or market controls.
- Repeated microcopy in uppercase with excessive tracking.
- Equal-weight feature grids that make every action look equally important.
- Listings, agents, reviews, partner logos, or investment returns before the capability is real.
- Ads inside Check results, Explore filters, evidence rows, maps, or source disclosures.
