Privacy at KoreaHomeGuide

You can use Rent Check, Explore, and browser-saved comparisons without creating an account. This notice separates information kept in your browser from information sent to KoreaHomeGuide or a service provider.

Who is responsible

KoreaHomeGuide operates this website and is responsible for the processing described here. The privacy contact is the KoreaHomeGuide operator at hello@koreahomeguide.com.

Saved homes stay in your browser

A saved comparison can contain your label, district, housing type, deposit, monthly rent, floor area, verdict, evidence level, comparison counts, and data period. It is stored in this browser using local storage only after you click save. KoreaHomeGuide does not receive it through the saved-homes feature.

You can save up to eight comparisons. Each expires after 90 days, and you can remove one or clear all at any time. Clearing site data or using another browser or device also removes access to them. Do not put a landlord, broker, exact unit, phone number, or other identifying information in a label.

Information you choose to submit

If you submit the email form after agreeing to its privacy notice, we receive your email address and the rent-check context attached to the request. That context can include district, housing type, deposit, monthly rent, floor area, verdict, evidence level, comparable count, data period, calculated values, source page, campaign parameters, and referring website host. If you ask for help, we also receive your message.

We use this information to save and respond to your request, provide the rental guidance you requested, prevent abuse, and improve the service. We do not sell it. We do not send promotional marketing email without separate consent. Please do not submit passwords, identification numbers, bank details, precise addresses, or other sensitive information.

Analytics choices

Google Analytics does not load unless you select “Allow analytics.” If allowed, we measure page paths, referral and campaign context, browser or device signals, and privacy-reduced interaction events. We do not intentionally send email addresses, labels, rent amounts, help messages, or exact unit information to Analytics. Select “Privacy choices” in the footer to change your choice. Essential-only use remains available.

Retention, rights, and deletion

Submitted lead and help-request records are deleted or anonymized after 12 months unless needed for an active response or legal obligation. Browser-saved comparisons expire after 90 days. Provider logs and Analytics data follow the relevant provider and account settings.

You may ask to access, correct, suspend processing of, or delete information associated with your email. Send the request from that address to hello@koreahomeguide.com. We may request reasonable verification to protect you from an unauthorized request.

Security and children

We use request validation, access controls, rate limits, and restricted credentials appropriate to this service. No online service can promise absolute security. KoreaHomeGuide is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes
